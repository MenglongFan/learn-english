Keyword · 关键词 — 单个词的口语用法

boost

a quick increase of energy

口语里高频指「提一把」，a little energy boost = 提提神；可作动词：Coffee boosts me up。别只会用 improve。

I need a little energy boost today.
tap

touch to pay with a card

刷卡/手机贴上去滴一下就是 tap。美国日常支付语境的高频动词，别用 touch。

You can tap it right here.
unsweetened

with no added sugar

un- 前缀产品的标准构词：unsweetened（无糖）/ sweetened（加糖）。点单时店员必问的一对。

Sweetened or unsweetened vanilla?

Phrase · 短语/搭配 — 多词固定搭配，字面义 ≈ 实际义

Can I get ~ instead of ~?

swap one thing for another

替换式点单万能句：Can I get almond milk instead of regular milk?；instead of 后接名词，口语比 I would like to replace... 自然一百倍。

Can I get almond milk instead of regular milk?
It went through.

the payment was successful

支付/提交成功的地道说法，go through = 顺利通过。别用 success。退款失败同理：The refund didn't go through.

Perfect, it went through.
Have a good one.

casual goodbye, have a good day

美式收尾万能再见，比 Have a good day 更随意，店员/顾客互相都用。one 指代 day。

Thanks! Have a good one.
totally worth it

definitely worth the cost

totally 在美口语中是纯强调词（= absolutely），无字面义。It's totally worth it = 太值了。

I come here a lot, so that sounds totally worth it.

Idiom · 习语 — 字面直译会闹笑话

Tell me about it.

I agree completely, I know

字面「跟我说说」≠ 实际义。实际是「你算说到点子上了/可不是嘛」，表达强烈同感。字面直译会闹笑话 → 归 Idiom。

It's so hot out today. — Tell me about it.
Don't we all?

rhetorical: yes, everyone does

反问句表「谁不是呢」。I need a little energy boost. — Don't we all? 直译「我们不都是吗」会很怪，实际是附和+自嘲。

I need a little energy boost today. — Don't we all?

Grammar Point · 语法点 — 影响地道度的口语语法

I'd like / I'll take

contracted polite ordering forms

点单句式的缩略是地道的分水岭：I would like → I'd like（弱读几乎听不到 would），I will have → I'll have/I'll take。全称形式在真实口语里几乎不存在。

弱读后 I'd like 听感接近 /aɪd laɪk/，跟读时注意 d 不要吞掉。

I'd like an iced latte, please.
That'll be $4.50.

that will be: stating the total

报总价的标准句式，that'll（that will）缩略。同类：You'll be all set. all set = 办好了，美国服务业高频。

That'll be $4.50. Here's my card.
